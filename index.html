How do I place an order?

Add a jar to your cart, tap Checkout and fill in your delivery details. You pay by Cash on Delivery. You can also message us on WhatsApp.

How can I track my order?

Use the order number shown after checkout on the Track Order page, with the phone number you ordered with.

Which sizes are available?

Each product shows its available sizes and prices. Sold-out sizes are marked on the product card.

What if I am not happy with my order?

Please read our Returns and Refunds policy or message us on WhatsApp.

Is this a medicine?

No. It is a natural product and is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any disease. Please consult a healthcare professional before use.
